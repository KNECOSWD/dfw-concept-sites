Expert advice on how you can reduce auto repair costs

At Best Price Tires & Auto, we strive to keep our customers loyal and happy — and that means keeping them out of the shop as much as possible. How can you keep the cost of car repairs down?

  • Always do your factory recommended maintenance
  • Don’t put off repairs which could lead to additional, more expensive repairs
  • Ask our service professionals for advice and for service options
  • Check on things like tire pressure and inflate immediately as needed
  • Combine errands and avoid extra trips you don’t need to take
