Talking to an automotive professional

We want to talk to you about your vehicle, and we definitely want to listen to what you have to say. After all, you’re the expert when it comes to what seems wrong with your car. When you come in for your auto repair estimate, be sure to tell us about anything you have observed — seen, heard, or felt — that is different.

If you get a chance, jot down some notes before you come in. What is your car doing (or not doing)? How is it handling? Are there any new noises you’ve been hearing? How is your vehicle’s performance — is it braking well and speeding up like it should?
