Irving & Dallas County family law lawyer.

The Law Office of Len Conner focuses on comprehensive divorce and family law representation. Len has spent his entire legal career on family law matters, identifying and implementing the best solutions for his clients.

Our office partners with psychologists, social workers, financial advisors, private investigators, and tax professionals. To set up a confidential meeting, call 972-445-1500. Our office is in Irving; we represent clients in surrounding cities and counties.
